## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **university student & open-source researcher** passionate at the intersection of **AI, Reinforcement Learning, and 3D Computer Graphics**.

- 🔬 Building **research-grade AI benchmarks** with honest, reproducible evaluation
- 🚀 Exploring **3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS)** optimization and **urban mobility AI**
- 📊 Advocate for **reproducible research** — strict train/test splits, checked-in reference metrics, and honest negative results
- 🎯 Believer in **rigorous experimentation** over hype-driven claims

## 🔬 Research Philosophy

> **"Reproducibility is not optional."**

Every benchmark in my repositories includes:
- ✅ Checked-in reference metrics for validation
- ✅ Strict temporal / leakage-safe data splits
- ✅ Honest negative results — *not every idea works*
- ✅ Paired statistical tests — *not just point estimates*
- ✅ Transparent limitations documented alongside results
